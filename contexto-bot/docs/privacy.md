# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** September 29, 2026

This policy explains what the Contexto Discord bot ("the bot"), run by [OPERATOR NAME] ("we"),
collects, why it collects it, and how you can delete it.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Discord **user ID** | To credit your guesses and points |
| Words you guess in the game channel, with their rank and time | To run the round, the live board and the guess list |
| Rounds you win | To calculate points and leaderboards |
| **Server ID**, the game **channel ID**, an optional **manager role ID** and the game settings | To know where and how to run the game |
| IDs of messages the bot posts | To update the live board |

## What we don't collect

- **Messages other than guesses.** Discord gives the bot the text of messages in channels it can
  see. The bot only looks at messages in the game channel, and only to check whether a message is
  a single-word guess. Nothing else is stored.
- Usernames, avatars, email addresses, direct messages, or voice data.

Leaderboards and stats show your Discord mention. Discord fills in your current name when the
message is displayed. We don't store names.

## How we use it

We use the data only to run the game: ranking guesses, showing boards and leaderboards, and
answering `/stats`. We don't sell it or share it with third parties, and we don't use it for
advertising or to train AI models.

## Where it's stored

The data is kept in a database on the machine that hosts the bot, run by [OPERATOR NAME]. Only
the operator can access it.

## How long we keep it

- **Your data:** until you delete it (see below), or until the server's data is deleted.
- **A server's data:** deleted automatically when the bot is removed from the server.

## Deleting your data

- Run **`/contexto forget-me`** to delete your guesses and points in every server, straight away.
- A server manager can remove the bot from the server to delete all of that server's data.
- You can also email [CONTACT EMAIL] to ask for deletion or a copy of your data.

## Children

The bot is for people who meet Discord's minimum age. We don't knowingly collect data from
anyone younger.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
